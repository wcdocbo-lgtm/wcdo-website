About Us

Who We Are

Empowering Communities Since 2011

WOTE Community Development Organisation (WCDO) is a non-political, non-religious, non-profit Community-Based Organisation registered in the Republic of Kenya.

We believe in the power of communities to transform their own lives when given the right support and resources.

Our Work

Our Pillars of Work

We work across five key pillars to create lasting change in our communities.

Health & Rehabilitation

Promoting community health through awareness, rehabilitation services, and accessible healthcare initiatives.

Learn More

Inclusive Education

Ensuring quality education for all, including marginalized groups, through scholarships and support programs.

Learn More

Inclusive Livelihood

Creating sustainable economic opportunities through skills training, entrepreneurship, and income generation.

Learn More

Social Inclusion

Breaking barriers and building bridges to ensure everyone, regardless of gender or disability, has a voice.

Learn More

Empowerment

Building capacity and leadership skills to enable communities to take charge of their own development.

Learn More

Climate Action & Environmental Sustainability

Building community resilience to climate change through locally-led adaptation, environmental restoration, and sustainable livelihood practices.
